# Design system

- Main ink: `#183F57`; secondary: `#235A7A`; mint: `#71C4B8`; paper: `#F5F7F9`.
- Original truck/check icon; no examination authority artwork or implied endorsement.
- System sans-serif with Japanese and Korean sans-serif fallbacks. Keep Korean and English words intact; use short semantic headline lines.
- Introduce offline study, show a clearly fictional timeline example, explain the five subjects, then provide official references and support.
- Mobile first. No store download or payment action while the product is preparing for release.
